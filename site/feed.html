The extra duties shown are Section 301 (China) and Section 232 (steel, aluminum, copper) only. They exclude AD/CVD, IEEPA/reciprocal duties, exclusions and quotas (coverage notes). On-chain shows the live TariffOracle rate where one exists. Every other line is part of the Merkle dataset (gas plan), which is not yet published on-chain. Search runs in your browser and sends no data anywhere. This is not legal advice; check CBP/USITC before you file.

Optional (admin): deploy the TFS-1 adapter

A read-only TariffFeedAdapter that wraps the live oracle so it speaks TFS-1 (ERC-165, batch reads, source id). It has no owner and cannot change rates. The oracle itself is not touched. Deployed through the CREATE2 factory, so it works from a smart wallet. Costs mainnet gas.
